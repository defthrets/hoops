HOOPS
by spitmux

Shooting hoops on the basketball court in Chamberlain Hills, on the court's own hoops.


INSTALL

You need ScriptHookV and ScriptHookVDotNet 3 (3.9 or newer).

Drag the "scripts" folder from this zip into your GTA V folder -- the one with GTA5.exe in
it. That puts these in place:

    scripts\Hoops.dll
    scripts\Hoops.ini
    scripts\Hoops\icons\

Works on GTA V Legacy and Enhanced.


PLAYING

Go to the basketball court in Chamberlain Hills, off Carson Avenue. There is a faint orange
ring on the court: walk into it and press E (D-pad right on a pad) to pick up the ball.

    Hold the attack button     the meter runs up and back down while you hold it
    Let go                      shoot
    Left stick, while holding   fine-tune your aim
    Backspace / B               put the ball down

Look where you want to shoot. While the meter runs, a line comes out of the ball and rises
and stretches with it -- it fades out before it comes down, so where the ball lands is yours
to judge. Down through either hoop is two points, three from beyond the arc. You can throw it
as far as you like.

Walk off the court and the game ends.


WITH POSTED UP

Posted Up has this same court built in. If both are installed, Hoops stands down so there
are not two games on one court. To run both anyway, set StandDownForPostedUp=false in
Hoops.ini.
